---
title: Sessions
description: Record, upload, and review materials from an individual meeting.
---

You can create a session in two ways: record a meeting in Sessio or upload an existing audio recording.

After processing, the session page contains:

- **Summary** — key themes, agreements, risks, and a plan for the next meeting;
- **Transcript** — therapist and client turns with timestamps;
- **Note** — an automatically saved draft structured for the selected approach.

You can also play the recording, ask a question with **Ask Sessio**, share materials, export the note, or delete the session.

All meetings are listed under **Sessions** and in the **Sessions** tab of the client profile.
