title: Online booking

---

Online booking gives a client a link where they choose a free time, and you confirm the request.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Schedule → Online booking**.

## How to set it up

1. Turn on online booking and set when you're available (the **When I work** section).
2. Copy the public link and send it to your client.
3. The client picks a free time — the request comes to you.

## What you'll see

New booking requests appear in the schedule. You can **confirm** or **decline** a request; on confirmation, the appointment is placed in the schedule.

## If it didn't work

- **The client doesn't see any free time.** Check your working hours in the **When I work** section.
- **A request disappeared.** The slot may already have been taken by another appointment — suggest the client pick a different one.
