Once a session is processed, it gets a page with several tabs. From here you can open the note, read the transcript, view the summary, ask a question about the session, and share materials.

## What's inside

- [Session note](/after-session/note) — a note draft that is saved automatically.
- [Transcript and roles](/after-session/transcript) — the session text turn by turn; this is also where you fix mixed-up roles.
- [Session summary](/after-session/summary) — the main themes, agreements, and a plan for the next meeting.
- [Ask Sessio](/after-session/ask-sessio) — questions about a specific session.
- [Share materials with a supervisor](/after-session/share-supervisor) — a de-identified version for a supervisor.
- [Export or delete a session](/after-session/export-delete) — save as PDF/Markdown or delete.
