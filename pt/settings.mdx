---
title: Settings
description: Manage your account, permissions, language, notifications, and privacy.
---

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Settings**.

Main sections:

- **Account** — profile, approach, email, and account deletion;
- **Preferences** — theme, language, dates, sounds, and notifications;
- **Permissions** — microphone, Screen Recording, and notification access;
- **Archived clients** — restore or permanently delete clients;
- **Help** — guides, bug reports, and feature suggestions;
- **Data and privacy** — a plain-language overview of how session data is handled;
- **About** — your Sessio version and update checks.

Select **Back to app** to return to the main screen.
