Your first session covers the whole path: you add a client, record or upload a meeting, and open a finished note draft.

## Where to find it

The first time you launch Sessio, sign in with Google, Apple, or Email and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

![Sessio sign-in screen: continue with Google, Apple, or Email](/images/sign-in.png)

Then complete a short profile setup — your name, practice country, and approach.

![Profile setup on first launch of Sessio](/images/onboarding.png)

After that, the welcome screen opens with **Record now** and **Choose file** buttons and the demo client “Anna” — you can try everything on it without using real data.

![Sessio welcome screen: Choose file, Record now, and the demo client Anna](/images/first-run-welcome.png)

## How to do it

1. Select **Add client** in the sidebar and enter a name.
2. Choose how to create the session:
   - to record a meeting, select **Start session**;
   - if you already have a recording, open the client's **Files** tab, upload the audio, and select **Create session**.
3. Wait while Sessio prepares the transcript, summary, and note draft.
4. Open the session and review the note.

## What you'll see

A new meeting appears in the client's session list with a processing status. When processing finishes, the transcript, summary, and note draft become available. The draft is saved automatically and can be edited at any time.

## If it didn't work

:::note
If processing does not finish, the session and your manual notes remain saved. You can retry processing while the source audio is still available.
:::

See [Upload and processing issues](/troubleshooting/processing) for more.
