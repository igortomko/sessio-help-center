---
title: Ways to add a session
description: Record a meeting or upload an existing audio recording.
---

You can create a session in two ways: record a meeting directly in Sessio or upload an existing audio recording. Either way, Sessio then prepares the transcript, summary, and note draft.

- [Record a session](/record-upload/record) — record through the microphone and call audio.
- [Upload an existing recording](/record-upload/upload) — create a session from an MP3, M4A, or WAV.
- [How session processing works](/record-upload/processing) — what the statuses mean after recording or upload.
- [Recording issues](/troubleshooting/recording) — if recording doesn't start or is interrupted.
