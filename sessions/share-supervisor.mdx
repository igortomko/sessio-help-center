---
title: Share materials with a supervisor
description: Prepare and copy de-identified session materials.
---

Sessio helps prepare session materials to share with a supervisor.

1. Open the relevant session.
2. Select **Share**.
3. Open the **Supervisor** tab.
4. Review the preview.
5. Copy the text or link.

In the supervisor version, Sessio removes names and identifying details. Always review the preview before sending it: wording from the session itself may still identify someone indirectly.

:::warning
Share materials only through a channel agreed with the client and supervisor. Automatic de-identification does not replace your own review of the text.
:::
