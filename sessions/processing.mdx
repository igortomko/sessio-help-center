---
title: How session processing works
description: Understand the statuses shown after recording or upload.
---

After recording ends, Sessio uploads the audio, prepares the transcript, and creates the session materials.

Main statuses:

- **Uploading audio** — the recording is being sent from your Mac;
- **Preparing transcript** — the audio has been received and is waiting for processing;
- **Preparing draft** — the transcript is ready and Sessio is creating the session materials;
- **Ready to review** — the summary, transcript, and note are available;
- **Processing error** — you can retry processing.

The note draft is saved automatically as soon as it is ready. Manual notes remain available even if transcription fails.

:::note
You can retry processing while the source audio is still available. If it has already been deleted, Sessio will explain that processing cannot be retried.
:::
