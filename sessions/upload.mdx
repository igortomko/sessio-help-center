---
title: Upload an existing recording
description: Create a session from an MP3, M4A, or WAV file.
---

You can upload an existing recording without granting microphone or Screen Recording access.

## From the session list

1. Open **Sessions**.
2. Select **Upload session**.
3. Choose an MP3, M4A, or WAV file.
4. Select the client.
5. Wait for the upload to finish.

## From a client profile

1. Open the client profile and go to **Files**.
2. Upload the audio recording.
3. Select **Create session**.
4. Choose the meeting date.

The session date appears in the client's history and cannot be in the future.

After upload, Sessio prepares a transcript, summary, and note draft. You can close the page while processing continues in the background.
