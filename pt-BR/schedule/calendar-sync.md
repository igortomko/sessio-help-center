---
title: Calendar sync
description: How to show your Sessio appointments in Apple or Google Calendar.
---

You can show your Sessio appointments in your Apple or Google calendar alongside everything else.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Schedule → Sync**.

## How to connect

1. Click **Add to Apple Calendar** or **Add to Google Calendar**.
2. Confirm adding the subscription calendar in your calendar app.

Sessio only adds its own appointments and does not read or change other events in your calendar.

## What you'll see

Your Sessio appointments appear in the selected calendar. Updates are pulled in automatically through the subscription link.

## If it didn't work

- **The link ended up in the wrong place.** Use **Replace link** to issue a new one and disable the old one.
- **Events don't appear.** Check that the subscription calendar has been added, and give your calendar app time to update.
