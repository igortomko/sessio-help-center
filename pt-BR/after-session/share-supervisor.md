---
title: Share materials with a supervisor
description: How to prepare and copy a de-identified version of a session.
---

Sessio helps you prepare session materials to share with a supervisor.

## Where to find it

Open a session and click **Share**.

## How to do it

1. In the **Share** dialog, select the **Supervisor** tab.
2. Review the text preview.
3. Copy the text or the link.
4. Send it through an agreed channel.

## What you'll see

Two tabs: **Client** (with the client's real name) and **Supervisor**. In the supervisor version, Sessio removes names and identifying details. There may be a note about wordings worth checking against the summary.

## If it didn't work

- **Recognizable details remain in the text.** Automatic de-identification does not catch everything: wording within the session can indirectly point to a person. Review the preview and edit the text before sending.
- **Copying doesn't work.** Try again; if that doesn't help, copy the text manually from the preview.

:::warning
Share materials only through a channel agreed with your client and supervisor. Automatic de-identification does not replace your own review of the text.
:::
