---
title: Export or delete a session
description: Save session materials to your Mac or delete a meeting.
---

## Export

Open the session and select **Actions**. Two formats are available:

- **PDF** — a ready-to-read or printable document;
- **Markdown** — a text format you can move into other apps.

The export includes the session note and basic metadata: date, approach, and therapist name.

## Delete

Select **Actions → Delete session**. The associated note, transcript, and audio are deleted with the session. They cannot be restored after confirmation.

If you need to keep the text outside Sessio, export it first.
