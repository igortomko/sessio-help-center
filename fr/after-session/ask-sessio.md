---
title: Ask Sio
description: How to ask a question about a client or a session and get an answer linked to what was said.
---

**Sio** answers questions about a client or a single session. Answers link to quotes from the transcript, so you can check where each fact comes from.

## Where to find it

The question field sits in the bottom-right corner of every screen in the app, with Sio to its right. Click the field or press **⌘J**.

What you ask about depends on the screen:

- on a client page — all of that client's sessions;
- on a session page — that session.

To ask about something else, click the avatar on the left of the field and choose **This session**, the client with all sessions, or **Another client · or @name**. You can also start your question with **@** and a client's name.

## How to do it

1. Click the field or press **⌘J**.
2. Type a question or pick a suggestion, such as **What did we agree on last time?** or **Which tasks came up?**.
3. Click **Send**. To interrupt the answer, click **Stop answer**.

While Sio is answering, you see the steps: **Transcript read**, **Sessions and schedule checked**, **Writing the answer**. After the answer you can ask a follow-up in the same dialog or start a **New dialog**. Press **Esc** to collapse the dialog.

**Asking about an excerpt.** Select text in a transcript and click **Ask about this excerpt**. The excerpt is attached to your question.

## What you'll see

- **An answer with numbered sources.** Click a number to see the quote, the date, and who said it. **Open in transcript** takes you to that line.
- **Verification tags.** **differs from the quote** means the quote says otherwise, so check the statement. **no source** means the statement links to nothing said.
- **Short answers without the dialog.** For questions like **When is the next session?** or **How many sessions have there been?**, the answer appears right above the field and the dialog doesn't open.
- **Answer actions.** **Add to note** inserts the answer with quotes into the session note. If you asked about a client, it goes into the note of the latest session. **Copy** copies the answer. **Mark as inaccurate** reports a mistake to us.

Questions and answers don't change the Portrait or the summary. An answer gets into a note only when you click **Add to note**.

## If it didn't work

- **The transcript isn't ready yet.** Wait until the session finishes processing and ask again.
- **This client has no sessions yet.** Sio answers only from recorded or uploaded sessions.
- **No questions left for today.** The number of questions per day is limited. The message says when new ones arrive.
- **No connection.** Ask once the network is back.
- **The field is gone.** You probably chose **Hide until tomorrow**. Press **⌘J** to bring it back.
- **You don't want suggestions from Sio.** Right-click Sio and choose **No suggestions**. To get them back, choose **Turn on suggestions** in the same menu.

:::note
If the sessions don't cover your question, Sio says so instead of making up an answer.
:::
