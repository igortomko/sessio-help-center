**Schedule** helps you plan appointments and see your day, week, or month. It's a separate section of the app, not the same as a client's list of sessions.

## Where to find it

Click **Schedule** in the sidebar (or `⌘S`). Switch between the **List**, **Week**, and **Month** views in the top-right corner; the **Today** button and the arrows move through the period.

![Schedule in "Week" view with an hourly grid](/images/schedule-week.png)

## What you can do

- [Schedule and change an appointment](/schedule/appointments), set up a repeat, and cancel.
- [Sync your schedule with your calendar](/schedule/calendar-sync) — Apple or Google.
- [Set up online booking](/schedule/online-booking) so clients can pick a time themselves.
- Hide client names with **Hide client names** — handy when sharing your screen.
