---
title: Export or delete a session
description: How to save materials to your Mac or delete a meeting.
---

You can save a session's materials to a file or delete the meeting entirely.

## Where to find it

Open a session and select **Actions** (the "⋮" menu).

## How to export

Choose a format:

- **PDF** — a ready document for reading or printing;
- **Markdown** — a text format for moving into other apps.

The export includes the session note and the main metadata: date, approach, and the practitioner's name.

## How to delete

Select **Actions → Delete session**. The associated note, transcript, and audio are deleted along with the session. They cannot be recovered after you confirm.

## What you'll see

A PDF or Markdown file on your Mac when you export. When you delete, the session disappears from the client's list.

## If it didn't work

- **You need to save the text before deleting.** Export the session first, then delete — data cannot be recovered after you confirm.
