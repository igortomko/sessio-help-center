The **Summary** tab gathers the session's main themes into a short structure so you can quickly return to what matters after the meeting.

## Where to find it

Open a session and go to the **Summary** tab.

## What you'll see

Depending on what the recording contains, the summary may include:

- brief context;
- leading themes and affects;
- agreements and homework;
- risks;
- a plan for the next session.

These items are insights about the session. They are not a diagnosis or a recommendation and do not replace your understanding of the client: read them alongside the recording.

## How to do it

- Hover over an item to edit, delete, reorder, or add to it manually.
- Click the source icon next to an item to open the part of the recording it is based on.
- Copy the summary as a whole or in individual blocks.

## If it didn't work

- **There is no summary.** It appears after the transcript is ready — see [How a session is processed](/record-upload/processing).
- **An item is flagged as needing a check.** Verify it against the transcript using the source icon.
