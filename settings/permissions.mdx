---
title: macOS permissions
description: Why Sessio needs microphone and Screen Recording access.
---

Sessio uses macOS system permissions to record sessions.

- **Microphone** records your voice.
- **Screen Recording** provides access to system audio, such as the client's voice from Zoom or Google Meet.
- **Accessibility** synchronizes recording controls with the active meeting window.
- **Notifications** let you know when the note is ready.

Open **Settings → Permissions** and select **Allow** next to the relevant item. If the system prompt does not appear, open **macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

You may need to restart Sessio after enabling Screen Recording.

Microphone and Screen Recording access are not required to upload an existing audio file.
