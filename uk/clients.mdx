---
title: The client card
description: A client's contacts, portrait, session history, and materials in one place.
---

A client card brings together contacts, the portrait, session history, and uploaded files. Active clients appear in the sidebar, and you can drag them to change their order.

- [Add or edit a client](/clients/add-client) — name, contacts, and a private note.
- [Client portrait](/clients/overview) — context from processed sessions and files.
- [Client files](/clients/files) — recordings, questionnaires, documents, and previous notes.
- [Archive or delete a client](/clients/archive-delete) — how to remove a client from the list.
- [Record a session](/record-upload/record) — run a meeting with the selected client.
