title: Schedule an appointment

---

In the schedule, you can plan an appointment with a client, set up a repeat, and cancel or reschedule the meeting if needed.

## Where to find it

Open **Schedule** and click **Schedule** (or select a free slot in the grid).

![Empty schedule with the "Schedule" button](/images/schedule-empty.png)

## How to schedule

1. Click **Schedule**.
2. Choose a client, date, and time.
3. If needed, turn on **Repeat** — weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or yearly.
4. Save the appointment.

If Google Meet or Zoom is connected, a meeting link is created automatically for an online appointment. You can connect them in **Settings → Integrations**.

## How to cancel or reschedule

Open the appointment and select **Cancel** or **Cancel and reschedule**. For a recurring meeting, Sessio asks whether to cancel a single appointment or the whole series. You can bring a canceled appointment back with **Schedule again**.

## What you'll see

The appointment in the schedule grid. The **Hide client names** button hides names when you're sharing your screen.

## If it didn't work

- **The client isn't in the list.** First [add the client](/clients/add-client).
- **The meeting link isn't created.** Connect Google Meet or Zoom in **Settings → Integrations**.
