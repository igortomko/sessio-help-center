Short answers to what people ask most often. If yours isn't here, write to us through **Settings → Help Center** or in the [Telegram community](/start/navigation).

## Where do I find the note?

Open a session and go to the **Note** tab. The draft appears there after the transcript is ready. More: [Session note](/after-session/note).

## Are changes saved automatically?

Yes. The note draft is saved automatically, and so are your edits. You don't need to approve or manually save the note — you can edit it at any time.

## The note isn't saving?

Most likely everything is fine: the note saves itself, and a saved indicator appears next to it. If the note isn't saving, check your internet connection and wait a couple of seconds — your edits apply automatically. More: [Session note](/after-session/note).

## Why isn't my recording being processed?

Processing runs in the background and goes through stages: audio upload → transcription → summary → done. If it stopped on an error, click **Try again** or **Process again** while the source file is still saved. Check the file format and your internet connection. More: [How a session is processed](/record-upload/processing) and [Upload and processing problems](/troubleshooting/processing).

## How do I fix mixed-up roles?

On the **Transcript** tab, change the speaker of an individual turn or use **Swap therapist and client** for the whole session, then prepare the note again. More: [Transcript and roles](/after-session/transcript).

## How is the summary different from the note?

The **Summary** is the main themes, agreements, and plan for the session. The **Note** is a draft based on your approach that you edit and keep. Both open on the session page.

## Does Sessio make a diagnosis?

No. Sessio prepares a transcript, a summary, and a note draft. Insights are not a diagnosis or a recommendation; decisions stay with you.

## Which file formats can I upload?

Audio — mp3, m4a, wav. Documents and images — pdf, txt, docx, doc, csv, jpg, png. Audio can be turned into a session with **Create session** or kept with **Keep as file**. More: [Client files](/clients/files).

## Can I record without choosing a client?

Yes. Start recording and link a client later. More: [Record a session](/record-upload/record).

## What happens to the audio after processing?

The transcript, summary, and note are saved in your account. The original recording is kept for a limited time after processing. You can delete a session, a client, or your account at any time. More: [Data and privacy](/settings/data-privacy).

## Do I need the client's consent to record?

Sessio doesn't add a separate consent screen and doesn't ask for confirmation inside the app. How you arrange recording with your client is up to you, based on your practice.

## How do I save the note outside Sessio?

Export the session to PDF or Markdown via **Actions**. More: [Export or delete a session](/after-session/export-delete).
