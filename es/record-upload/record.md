title: Record a session
description: Start, pause, and finish a meeting recording.
---

Sessio records a meeting directly on your Mac: your voice through the microphone and the client's voice through the call audio (Zoom, Google Meet, and others).

## Where to find it

You can start recording several ways: the **Start session** button in the sidebar, from search (`⌘K`), from the schedule, from the Sessio panel in the menu bar, or through the **Call detected** prompt when Sessio notices a call.

## How to do it

1. Select **Start session**.
2. Choose a client, or start without one and attach it later.
3. On your first recording, grant access to the **Microphone** and **Screen Recording**.
4. Start recording. During the meeting you can pause and resume it.
5. Stop the recording — Sessio saves the audio and starts processing.

![Recording access screen: Microphone and Call audio](/images/macos-permissions.png)

## What you'll see

Sessio records two sources: the **Microphone** (your voice) and **Call audio** (the client's voice). On macOS, access to call audio is called “Screen Recording.” After you stop, upload and processing continue in the background — you can keep using the app.

## If it didn't work

If the connection dropped, the recording stays on this Mac. You can retry the upload from the Sessio panel in the menu bar. Do not delete local files until the upload finishes. See [Recording issues](/troubleshooting/recording) for more.
