The **Transcript** tab shows the therapist's and client's turns linked to the recording. This is also where you fix roles if Sessio mixed up who is speaking.

## Where to find it

Open a session and go to the **Transcript** tab.

## How to do it

In the transcript you can:

- search the text with `⌘F`;
- jump to a specific point in the recording;
- edit the text of an individual turn;
- change the speaker of a single turn with **Change speaker**;
- swap roles across the whole session with **Swap therapist and client**.

## How to fix mixed-up roles

If roles are mixed up in a single turn, change its speaker. If they are mixed up across the whole session, use **Swap therapist and client**; repeating the action restores the original order. After you fix them, Sessio offers to prepare the note again.

## What you'll see

Turns with the correct roles. Summary items can link to specific parts of the transcript, so it's faster to check what a given wording is based on.

## If it didn't work

- **There is no transcript, or it isn't ready.** See [How a session is processed](/record-upload/processing).
- **Note edits disappeared after swapping roles.** Preparing the note again may overwrite manual changes — copy any important text in advance.
